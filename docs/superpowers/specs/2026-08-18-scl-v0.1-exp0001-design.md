# SCL v0.1 / EXP-0001 — Finite Semantic Crystallization Design

## Purpose

Semantic Crystallization Lab (SCL) tests a bounded engineering question: can a finite source passage be transformed through a hierarchy of newly defined reusable lexemes/operators so that the visible representation becomes progressively shorter while an explicit semantic expansion contract preserves a declared set of source invariants?

This experiment is a **finite demonstration**, not a proof of the stronger cardinality claims in the source theory.

## Research distinction

SCL separates two different operations:

1. **Address compression** — a label points to an external payload. Example: `X = whole article`. This is a control condition, not evidence of structural semantic compression.
2. **Structural semantic crystallization** — a lexeme/operator expands through a self-contained, typed lexicon into lower-level semantic structure; lower-level components must also be reusable in at least one novel composition that was not copied from the source passage.

A terminal one-word lexeme is permitted only as a **context-bound crystallization**. Its definition cost remains visible in the metrics.

## Source basis

EXP-0001 uses a 358-code-point Chinese passage selected verbatim from *內容信息上下界無限原理：認識論猜想*. The selected passage contains four main structural ideas:

- semantic/topological equivalence can coexist with different cognitive/geodesic experience;
- semantic accessibility is proposed to be bounded by physical reality rather than language alone;
- finite characters are used to probe a larger meaning space;
- novelty is associated with reaching a new meaning topology rather than only replacing surface characters.

## Canonical semantic IR

The first experiment does not attempt automatic natural-language understanding. The source passage is manually decomposed into explicit semantic invariants. These invariants are the evaluation target.

Canonical atoms:

- `REP(x)` — $x$ is a representation.
- `MEAN(x,m)` — representation $x$ reaches/expresses meaning configuration $m$.
- `ISO(x,y,m)` — $x$ and $y$ are meaning-equivalent with respect to $m$.
- `PATH(x,m,o,p)` — observer $o$ reaches $m$ from $x$ through cognitive path $p$.
- `DIFF(p,q)` — paths $p$ and $q$ are experientially different.
- `BOUND(m,b)` — accessible meaning $m$ is constrained by boundary $b$.
- `FINITE(x)` — the representation carrier $x$ is finite.
- `PROBE(x,m)` — $x$ is used to probe $m$.
- `NEWTOPO(m)` — $m$ contains a newly reached meaning topology relative to the current language basis.

## Crystallization lexemes

EXP-0001 introduces namespace-scoped experimental lexemes. They are arbitrary labels; their semantics come only from the checked-in lexicon.

- `vek(x,m,b)` — finite representation $x$ probes meaning $m$ under boundary $b$.
- `narel(x,y,m,o)` — two representations are meaning-equivalent while their cognitive paths differ for observer $o$.
- `vesh(x,m,b)` — representation $x$ reaches meaning $m$ under admissibility boundary $b$.
- `qevra` — the context-bound terminal crystallization for the EXP-0001 proposition bundle.

`qevra` is **not** treated as proof of net compression by itself. It is evaluated together with the complete lexicon cost and expansion tree.

## Compression chain

The experiment stores multiple levels:

- `L0` — original Chinese source passage.
- `L1` — concise controlled Chinese paraphrase.
- `L2` — semantic IR using base atoms.
- `L3` — IR using reusable composite operators (`vek`, `narel`, `vesh`).
- `L4` — context-bound terminal lexeme `qevra`.

The desired surface trend is:

$$
|L_{i+1}| < |L_i|
$$

for the checked-in EXP-0001 levels, while semantic invariant recall remains:

$$
R_i = \frac{|I_0 \cap I_i|}{|I_0|} = 1
$$

for the declared invariants.

## Dictionary-aware accounting

Surface shrinkage alone is insufficient. SCL records:

$$
C_{surface}(L_i) = \frac{|L_0|}{|L_i|}
$$

and dictionary-aware cost:

$$
K_i = |L_i| + |D_i|
$$

where $D_i$ is the cumulative UTF-8 lexicon/definition payload required to interpret that level without the original prose.

The experiment is allowed to show negative first-use net compression. That is an informative result, not a failure of the harness.

For a repeated corpus of $N$ documents sharing the same dictionary, the amortized cost is:

$$
K_i^{(N)} = |D_i| + \sum_{j=1}^{N}|L_{i,j}|
$$

This distinction is essential: vocabulary invention can be expensive on first use and beneficial only after reuse.

## Anti-cheat gates

EXP-0001 fails structural status if any of the following occurs:

1. a crystallized lexeme expands directly to the original source prose;
2. expansion requires reading `source.zh.txt`;
3. the terminal lexeme has no typed decomposition into lower-level semantic units;
4. none of the composite operators can be reused in a novel non-source composition;
5. a reported compression ratio excludes required lexicon cost without explicitly calling itself surface-only.

## Expansion contract

`expandLexeme()` recursively expands lexemes into semantic IR only. It does not reconstruct the original Chinese prose. Exact prose restoration is intentionally outside the v0.1 contract because storing the prose inside a dictionary would reduce the experiment to pointer/macro compression.

## Generativity check

At least one checked-in novel composition must be expressible using the new operators without introducing a new definition. EXP-0001 uses a code-oriented example:

`narel(codeA, codeB, sortMeaning, developer) + vesh(codeA, sortMeaning, computeBoundary)`

This is not part of the source passage; it demonstrates reuse of the operators as a semantic basis.

## EML-U relation

SCL and EML-U remain separate at v0.1:

- SCL discovers/tests semantic crystallizations and their evidence.
- EML-U provides a composite glyph and explicit crystallization/rebase mechanism.

A future adapter may export validated SCL lexemes as EML-U primitive candidates. v0.1 does not claim token-level identity or automatic primitive promotion.

## File/data integrity

- All new text source artifacts are UTF-8.
- Mathematical source uses only `$...$` and `$$...$$` delimiters.
- No Unicode-escape round trip is used for canonical text.
- Original uploaded documents are indexed by SHA-256 and included byte-for-byte in the downloadable session bundle.
- Experiment outputs are deterministic from the checked-in JSON and source files.

## Success criteria for EXP-0001

EXP-0001 is considered a successful engineering demonstration if:

1. the code verifies recursive expansion from `qevra` to the complete declared semantic invariant set without reading the source prose;
2. each compression level has measured code-point and UTF-8 byte length;
3. dictionary-aware cost is reported separately from surface compression;
4. composite operators pass at least one novel-composition reuse test;
5. the full test suite and experiment runner exit successfully;
6. generated metrics/report are reproducible.
