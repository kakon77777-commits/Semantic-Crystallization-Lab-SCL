# SCL v0.7 / EXP-0007 Design — Adaptive Epistemic Admission

## Goal

Test whether a history-conditioned trust policy can improve the plasticity–integrity tradeoff relative to fixed Q2/Q3 admission rules without reading current-event ground truth.

## Admission state

Each provenance root `r` has a Beta posterior:

$$
\rho_r = \frac{\alpha_r}{\alpha_r+\beta_r}.
$$

Feedback is delayed by one event. For a candidate with independent roots `R`, effective support is:

$$
W = \sum_{r\in R}\rho_r.
$$

The learned required support is:

$$
T = T_0 + w_R\,risk + w_N\,novelty + w_P\,recentPollution.
$$

Admission requires at least two distinct provenance roots and `W >= T`.

## Controls

- Static-Q2
- Static-Q3
- Oracle ceiling

## Failure probes

- correlated aliases sharing one provenance root;
- provenance reliability drift;
- emerging low-reputation genuine sources;
- historically trusted coalition compromise.

## Non-claims

The synthetic truth stream is an evaluation fixture. Beta reliability and the threshold equation are not proposed as production-optimal or universal epistemology.
