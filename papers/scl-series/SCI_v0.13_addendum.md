# SCI v0.13 Addendum — Finite Horizon, Deferral Budget, and Over-Rejection

EXP-0013 adds a bounded governance distinction to the SCI line:

$$
\text{breaking the defer attractor} \neq \text{balanced autonomous resolution}.
$$

A depth-2 inquiry rollout plus a hard cross-event deferral budget converts the EXP-0012 all-defer solution into active evidence acquisition and immediate decisions. In the formal fixture, A5 uses 22 probes, defers only 4/22 events and resolves 81.8% immediately.

The new failure mode is systematic conservatism: the same arm records zero false accepts but four false rejects. The autonomy problem therefore becomes a constrained plasticity problem. A future SCI governance layer should not optimize only total loss and deferral count; it should also bound rejection risk or enforce a minimum ability to admit genuine novelty.

This is an experimental addendum, not a universal theorem or production calibration.
